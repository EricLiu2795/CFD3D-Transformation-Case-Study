    Reorganise solver and IO sources into role-named files
    
    The source tree used a one-file-per-stage-per-backend naming scheme
    (BorderValuesCpp/Cuda, FluidSolverCpp/Cuda, ...) that made it hard to
    tell at a glance what each file was responsible for. Rename the files
    after what they do and drop the backend suffixes in favour of a serial/
    and a cuda/ subdirectory:
    
      - the host backend moves to CfdSolver/serial/ (boundary_conditions,
        momentum_eqs, poisson_sor, serial_solver);
      - the CUDA backend moves to CfdSolver/cuda/ (boundary_kernels,
        momentum_kernels, poisson_kernels, cuda_solver, cuda_runtime_check);
      - the shared pieces become solver_interface, field_init and cell_flags;
      - the IO and particle-tracer modules get descriptive names.
    
    Fold the two tiny string/filesystem helper modules into a single
    text_file_utils module, since they were always used together, and move
    the scenario-specific inflow boundary into its own scenario_boundary
    file so the general boundary code stands on its own.
    
    All #include directives, including those in the OpenCL and MPI backends,
    are updated to the new paths. No behaviour changes.
